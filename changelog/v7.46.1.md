## Release Notes

This release improves Jira form and workflow migration:

- Detail forms preserve Jira view/edit screen fields and their visibility and required settings, including Reporter, Environment, and Due Date.
- Reporter is included on the Create form only when configured on Jira's create screen.
- Custom fields follow their Jira field contexts for each project and issue type.
- Epic Name follows the actual Epic issue type; other types retain it only when configured on their screens.
- Transition forms preserve their own field visibility and required rules, including Resolution, without inheriting unrelated settings.
- Transition-only fields remain available as hidden detail fields.
- Migrated layouts avoid duplicate fields, including version fields, and preserve explicit Hidden or Required settings.
- Internal layout and workflow processing was updated for consistent field placement and configuration.
